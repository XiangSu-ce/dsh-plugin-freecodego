# FreeCodeGo Trademark and Fork Naming Policy

The source code of this repository is licensed under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3](LICENSE).
That license covers the **code only**. It does **not** grant any right to use the
**FreeCodeGo** name, logo, visual identity, or official distribution channels.

## What this means for forks

You may fork, modify, and redistribute the code under AGPL-3.0, provided you follow
these rules:

1. **Rename your distribution.** A fork must not be named "FreeCodeGo" or any
   confusingly similar name (including "FreeCodeGo CE", "FreeCodeGo X", or names
   differing only by TLD/subdomain). State clearly in your README that it is a fork
   of FreeCodeGo, with a link to this repository.
2. **Do not use the official branding.** The FreeCodeGo logo, wordmark, color
   identity, website, and app-store listings may not be reused by a fork.
3. **Do not point users at official infrastructure.** A fork must not present
   itself as the official FreeCodeGo gateway, update channel, support channel, or
   account system, and must not proxy or mirror official endpoints while claiming
   official status.
4. **Keep the license notices.** Preserve the AGPL-3.0 license text and the
   third-party notices, and mark your modifications as required by AGPL §5(a).

## What remains ours

- The name **FreeCodeGo** and its logo
- The official build signatures and release channels
- The official FreeCodeGo gateway service and account system (operated under the
  service terms, separate from this source license)

## Permissions

Naming a project "a fork of FreeCodeGo" in a descriptive sentence ("inspired by",
"based on FreeCodeGo") with a link back is allowed and encouraged. Anything beyond
that — commercial redistribution under the FreeCodeGo name, bundling, or official
partnerships — requires prior written permission from the project maintainers.

This policy follows the standard open-source practice of separating code licenses
from trademarks (the same model used by Firefox/Debian and VS Code/VSCodium).
